- Countryside huts/crypts/landmarks, open-world treasure chests and hermit side quests are generated in `buildWorld` (`story-terrain.ts`, steps 7b/9b/9c) and their rules live in pure `src/lib/story-quests.ts`; saves hold only `sideAsked/sideFound/sideDone/treasures` keys, so map tweaks never need stored lists.
- Story songs are authored scale-degree data in pure `src/lib/story-songs.ts` (`songFor` picks the song for a place) and `src/lib/story-music.ts` only plays them via Web Audio; the open world reports its song through `onSong`, so new places only need a `songFor` case and stay testable.
- Story worlds are authored on a 192×144 grid and stretched by `SCALE` (`sc()` in `story-terrain.ts`); village, labyrinth and building sizes are fixed constants there, so new sites must go through `sc()` to keep their relative spot as worlds grow.
- Elves are drawn from `elfRectsHD` (Scale2x of the 16×24 art); edit sprite art at 16×24 in `pixel-elf.ts`, keeping the head (rows 3-13) wider than the body (rows 14-18) because hair, ears, caps and gear layers are drawn to those rows. Hero-only look options sit past `NPC_LOOK_COUNTS`, so residents never get them.
- Story world terrain starts as a Minish-Cap-style area patchwork (`worldRegions`/`regionCell` in `story-terrain.ts`): a grid of biome areas fenced by tree/boulder walls with fixed openings, so roads, villages and mountains are built on top and reachability never depends on a road alone.
